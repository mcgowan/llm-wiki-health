# Overview

Dr. Mitch Rice presents insulin resistance as a common but rarely diagnosed driver of stubborn weight gain, fatigue, cravings, creeping blood sugar, and gradually rising blood pressure, especially in people over 50[^the-top-10-ways-to-reverse-insulin-resistance-do-HlVOzRmI3VI]. He describes it using a lock-and-key analogy: insulin is the key that lets blood sugar into cells, and resistance occurs when that lock "rusts," forcing the pancreas to secrete progressively more insulin to keep blood sugar looking normal on standard labs, even while chronically elevated insulin quietly promotes fat storage, cravings, energy crashes, and hypertension[^the-top-10-ways-to-reverse-insulin-resistance-do-HlVOzRmI3VI]. His central message is that the condition is fully reversible through consistent lifestyle changes rather than extreme dieting or intense exercise[^the-top-10-ways-to-reverse-insulin-resistance-do-HlVOzRmI3VI].

# The 10 strategies

- **Eat protein before carbs** at each meal, which he says slows sugar absorption; he cites a Journal of Diabetes Care study finding this meal-order change cut post-meal blood sugar spikes by up to 37% and insulin spikes by up to 57%[^the-top-10-ways-to-reverse-insulin-resistance-do-HlVOzRmI3VI].
- **Walk for 10 minutes after meals**, ideally within 30 minutes of finishing, since muscle absorbs glucose directly during activity; he states this can reduce post-meal glucose spikes by up to 30%[^the-top-10-ways-to-reverse-insulin-resistance-do-HlVOzRmI3VI].
- **Choose "smart carbs" over refined carbs** — replacing white bread, pastries, and sugary drinks with beans, lentils, sweet potatoes, berries, and whole oats paired with protein[^the-top-10-ways-to-reverse-insulin-resistance-do-HlVOzRmI3VI].
- **Build muscle via resistance training** 3-4 times weekly (bodyweight, bands, or light weights), since more muscle gives glucose more places to go[^the-top-10-ways-to-reverse-insulin-resistance-do-HlVOzRmI3VI].
- **Protect sleep**, aiming for 7-8 hours with a consistent wake time in a cool, dark room, since even one night of poor sleep raises cortisol and reduces insulin sensitivity[^the-top-10-ways-to-reverse-insulin-resistance-do-HlVOzRmI3VI].
- **Manage chronic stress**, using slow breathing (5 seconds in, 5 seconds out) for five minutes daily, because sustained cortisol elevation directly blunts insulin sensitivity[^the-top-10-ways-to-reverse-insulin-resistance-do-HlVOzRmI3VI].
- **Add magnesium glycinate** (240-600 mg in the evening), citing that roughly 80% of adults over 50 are deficient and that low magnesium worsens insulin binding, sleep, and cravings[^the-top-10-ways-to-reverse-insulin-resistance-do-HlVOzRmI3VI].
- **Don't skip breakfast**, since insulin sensitivity is highest in the morning and skipping it produces bigger spikes and crashes later in the day; also finish dinner 2-3 hours before bed[^the-top-10-ways-to-reverse-insulin-resistance-do-HlVOzRmI3VI].
- **Increase fiber intake** through beans, lentils, whole fruit (not juice), and chia or flax seeds, which slows sugar absorption into the bloodstream[^the-top-10-ways-to-reverse-insulin-resistance-do-HlVOzRmI3VI].
- **Lose a modest amount of visceral fat** — he states even 5-10 lb from the midsection can meaningfully restore insulin sensitivity, since visceral fat releases inflammatory signals that block insulin receptors[^the-top-10-ways-to-reverse-insulin-resistance-do-HlVOzRmI3VI].
