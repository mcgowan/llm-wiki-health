# Overview

Dr. Mitch Rice ranks five common fruits by their purported benefit to arterial health, from indirect cholesterol effects to what he describes as direct, physical reversal of arterial narrowing, and closes with the claim that combining several of these fruits' polyphenols produces a greater benefit than any single fruit eaten in bulk.[^5-incredibly-common-fruits-that-melt-clogged-art-qtaWvFZHEYs] The throughline across all five is that specific plant compounds (monounsaturated fat, flavonoids, resveratrol, anthocyanins, polyphenols) act on cholesterol particle size, endothelial function, nitric oxide production, or arterial wall thickness.[^5-incredibly-common-fruits-that-melt-clogged-art-qtaWvFZHEYs]

# The five fruits, ranked

- **5. Avocado**: monounsaturated fat similar to olive oil is said to shift LDL toward larger, less atherogenic particles and modestly raise HDL, working through cholesterol composition rather than acting on the artery wall directly. Suggested amount: about half to one avocado daily, ideally replacing a saturated fat source.[^5-incredibly-common-fruits-that-melt-clogged-art-qtaWvFZHEYs]
- **4. Citrus fruits**: hesperidin, concentrated in the white pith and inner membrane, is said to improve endothelial function and reduce arterial stiffness (measured by pulse wave velocity); daily orange juice is cited as providing a measurable benefit, though eating the whole fruit with pith is recommended as better. A specific caution: grapefruit interacts with the blood pressure drug amlodipine via liver enzyme inhibition and should be avoided by patients on that medication, whereas oranges, lemons, and limes do not carry that interaction.[^5-incredibly-common-fruits-that-melt-clogged-art-qtaWvFZHEYs]
- **3. Red/purple grapes**: resveratrol is said to improve endothelial function via increased nitric oxide availability, and quercetin in grapes is described as modestly lowering blood pressure and working synergistically with resveratrol. Suggested amount: about one cup of grapes daily.[^5-incredibly-common-fruits-that-melt-clogged-art-qtaWvFZHEYs]
- **2. Blueberries**: anthocyanins are said to raise nitric oxide production and improve flow-mediated dilation (arterial widening in response to blood flow), also supporting lower blood pressure. Suggested amount: about half to one cup daily, fresh or frozen.[^5-incredibly-common-fruits-that-melt-clogged-art-qtaWvFZHEYs]
- **1. Pomegranate**: ranked highest because, per a cited one-year study in patients with existing carotid artery narrowing, daily pomegranate juice was associated with a measurable reduction in arterial wall thickness and improved blood flow versus a control group whose narrowing progressed — described as the only fruit on the list shown to physically reduce existing arterial thickening rather than just improve a functional marker. Suggested amount: about 8 oz of pomegranate juice daily.[^5-incredibly-common-fruits-that-melt-clogged-art-qtaWvFZHEYs]

# The combination effect

Rice cites a study on combined polyphenol intake from multiple fruit sources finding that endothelial function improved more than would be predicted by summing each fruit's individual effect — a "synergistic" result he summarizes as "1 + 1 = 5." His practical takeaway is to rotate through several of these fruits across the week rather than concentrating on the single highest-ranked one.[^5-incredibly-common-fruits-that-melt-clogged-art-qtaWvFZHEYs]

# Caveats

Every specific study cited (Journal of the American Heart Association, American Journal of Clinical Nutrition, American Journal of Cardiology, Journal of Clinical Nutrition, and the unnamed combined-polyphenol study) is referenced by journal name only, with no authors, sample sizes, or links provided, so the reported effect sizes cannot be independently checked.
