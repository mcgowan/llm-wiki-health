# Overview

Dr. Mitch Rice proposes a 10-minute morning routine intended to reduce cancer risk in older adults, framed around a claim that a "landmark analysis" of modifiable lifestyle factors found roughly 40% of cancer cases, across the cancer types studied, are attributable to changeable behaviors such as physical activity, diet, and metabolic health.[^the-10-min-daily-habit-to-prevent-cancer-in-olde-BaQGYXt2P0g] The routine is presented as targeting three biological pathways he says matter for cancer development: immune surveillance by natural killer (NK) cells, insulin and insulin-like growth factor 1 (IGF-1) signaling that can promote tumor growth, and chronic inflammation.[^the-10-min-daily-habit-to-prevent-cancer-in-olde-BaQGYXt2P0g]

# The routine

- Minutes 0-2: paced breathing, inhaling through the nose for 4 seconds and exhaling through the mouth for 8 seconds, said to shift the body toward parasympathetic activity and boost NK cell activity that Rice describes as suppressed by overnight cortisol.[^the-10-min-daily-habit-to-prevent-cancer-in-olde-BaQGYXt2P0g]
- Minutes 3-7: five minutes of light walking immediately after the first meal of the day. The stated reasoning is that walking during the post-meal insulin spike activates muscle glucose transporters that pull sugar from the blood without requiring insulin, lowering both insulin and IGF-1 — hormones described as growth signals cancer cells can exploit.[^the-10-min-daily-habit-to-prevent-cancer-in-olde-BaQGYXt2P0g]
- Minutes 8-10: finishing the morning shower with about three minutes of cold water, said to trigger a large surge in norepinephrine that mobilizes NK cells and cytotoxic T cells into circulation while suppressing inflammatory cytokines such as TNF-alpha and IL-6. Rice cites a study he attributes to the journal PLOS One reporting higher NK cell counts and cytotoxic T cell activity in people who practice regular cold-water exposure.[^the-10-min-daily-habit-to-prevent-cancer-in-olde-BaQGYXt2P0g]

# Why it's emphasized for older adults

Rice argues the routine is especially relevant after age 50 because NK cell activity naturally declines with age, insulin sensitivity drops (prolonging post-meal insulin elevation), and low-grade chronic inflammation becomes more common — the three mechanisms the routine is designed to counteract.[^the-10-min-daily-habit-to-prevent-cancer-in-olde-BaQGYXt2P0g] He also recommends the supplement berberine as additional support for inflammation and insulin resistance.[^the-10-min-daily-habit-to-prevent-cancer-in-olde-BaQGYXt2P0g]

# Caveats

The central "40% of cancers are preventable" statistic and the cited PLOS One finding are described only vaguely, without specific study names, authors, or sample sizes, so they cannot be independently verified. The video includes an Amazon affiliate link for a berberine supplement and promotes a paid, waitlisted private community, both financial interests layered onto the health claims. Rice does explicitly caveat that the routine cannot guarantee cancer prevention.
