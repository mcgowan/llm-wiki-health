# Overview

Dr. Mitch Rice argues that standard lab panels fail to capture "biological aging" and presents six alternative measurements he says better predict long-term mortality and disease risk: post-meal blood sugar response, single-leg balance, sleep depth and consistency, grip strength, resting heart rate trend, and waist-to-height ratio[^6-longevity-signals-that-predict-death-better-th--jcig9d6OlY]. His overall claim is that most of these can be self-tested in minutes and can reveal problems years before they would appear on routine blood work[^6-longevity-signals-that-predict-death-better-th--jcig9d6OlY].

# The six signals

- Post-meal (postprandial) glucose spikes above 140 mg/dL at 90 minutes, even alongside normal fasting glucose and A1c, are framed as an early, silent driver of vascular and neurological damage, best tracked with a continuous glucose monitor or finger-stick testing[^6-longevity-signals-that-predict-death-better-th--jcig9d6OlY].
- Single-leg balance held for at least 10 seconds is linked, via a cited study in the British Journal of Sports Medicine, to an 84% higher 10-year all-cause mortality risk in people unable to hold it, reflecting integration of the vestibular, visual, and proprioceptive systems[^6-longevity-signals-that-predict-death-better-th--jcig9d6OlY].
- Sleep depth and consistency (not just duration) are tied to nightly glymphatic clearance of amyloid beta and tau, with a cited study in the journal Science reporting that even one night of sleep deprivation raised amyloid beta accumulation in memory-related brain regions[^6-longevity-signals-that-predict-death-better-th--jcig9d6OlY].
- Grip strength, per a cited Lancet study of nearly 140,000 people across 17 countries, predicted cardiovascular, respiratory, and all-cause mortality more reliably than blood pressure, reflecting overall neuromuscular integrity[^6-longevity-signals-that-predict-death-better-th--jcig9d6OlY].
- A rising resting heart rate trend over time, even within the "normal" 50-70 bpm range, was independently linked to higher all-cause mortality in a cited study of over 29,000 people in the journal Heart[^6-longevity-signals-that-predict-death-better-th--jcig9d6OlY].
- Waist-to-height ratio (waist under half of height) is presented, via a cited Journal of the American College of Cardiology analysis and a New England Journal of Medicine study of over 350,000 people, as more predictive of cardiovascular death than BMI, reflecting visceral fat's inflammatory activity[^6-longevity-signals-that-predict-death-better-th--jcig9d6OlY].

# Caveats

The studies referenced (British Journal of Sports Medicine, Science, Lancet, Heart, JACC, and NEJM) are named only by journal, without authors, publication years, or enough detail to independently verify the exact findings as summarized; the video also promotes Rice's free "metabolic blueprint" opt-in resource[^6-longevity-signals-that-predict-death-better-th--jcig9d6OlY].
