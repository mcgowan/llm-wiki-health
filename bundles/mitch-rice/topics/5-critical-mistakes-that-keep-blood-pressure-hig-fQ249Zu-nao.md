# Overview

Dr. Mitch Rice argues that people who try to lower blood pressure naturally and fail are usually not lazy but are focusing on the wrong levers, since blood pressure is governed by multiple interacting systems — blood vessels, kidneys, hormones, the nervous system, and sleep — not diet alone.[^5-critical-mistakes-that-keep-blood-pressure-hig-fQ249Zu-nao] He walks through five specific mistakes he sees repeatedly in his own patients, especially those over 60, and offers a smaller substitute action for each.[^5-critical-mistakes-that-keep-blood-pressure-hig-fQ249Zu-nao]

# The five mistakes

- **Treating blood pressure as only a diet problem**: cutting salt and processed foods helps, but a person can eat well and still have high blood pressure if sleep, stress, or arterial stiffness are unaddressed. He advises using diet as one part of a broader plan (whole foods, adequate protein, potassium-rich foods, less processed sugar) rather than expecting it alone to normalize numbers.[^5-critical-mistakes-that-keep-blood-pressure-hig-fQ249Zu-nao]
- **Ignoring the nervous system**: a nervous system stuck in "fight or flight" keeps heart rate and vessel tone elevated, which he says can blunt medication effectiveness; he states one cannot "out-diet or out-supplement a stressed nervous system." Suggested countermeasures include slow breathing, consistent sleep timing, gentle daily movement, and quiet moments during the day.[^5-critical-mistakes-that-keep-blood-pressure-hig-fQ249Zu-nao]
- **Doing the wrong type of exercise**: pushing too hard, especially past age 60, raises stress hormones and increases recovery demand, which can keep the body in a stressed state without lowering blood pressure. He recommends gentler, sustainable options instead — walking, light resistance training, isometric holds, and short daily routines.[^5-critical-mistakes-that-keep-blood-pressure-hig-fQ249Zu-nao]
- **Fixating on single readings**: because readings naturally fluctuate with stress, caffeine, pain, and movement, reacting to one high number can itself raise adrenaline and blood pressure. His suggested method: measure at the same times daily (about 20 minutes after waking and just before bed), sit quietly 5 minutes first, take two readings, record the lower one, and track trends over 7–14 days.[^5-critical-mistakes-that-keep-blood-pressure-hig-fQ249Zu-nao]
- **Trying to change everything at once**: attempting diet, exercise, sleep, supplements, and stress reduction simultaneously tends to cause burnout and abandonment within weeks. He recommends adopting one small habit at a time and adding another only once it sticks.[^5-critical-mistakes-that-keep-blood-pressure-hig-fQ249Zu-nao]

# Why this matters more after 60

Rice notes that arteries stiffen and recovery slows with age, making regulation and consistency — not perfection — the realistic goal for older adults managing blood pressure naturally.[^5-critical-mistakes-that-keep-blood-pressure-hig-fQ249Zu-nao]
