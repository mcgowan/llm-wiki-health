# Overview

Dr. Mitch Rice presents a 10-item protocol he says he uses with patients to lower blood pressure naturally, framing hypertension as a "silent killer" that raises long-term risk of stroke, heart attack, kidney disease, heart failure, and cognitive decline even though patients typically feel fine[^i-helped-6-522-people-get-off-blood-pressure-med-jjW5msWyFio]. His overall argument is that blood pressure control is a systems problem — reflecting metabolic health, nervous system tone, sleep, muscle mass, and daily movement — rather than something fixed by a single shortcut, and that even a 5-point drop in blood pressure meaningfully reduces heart attack and stroke risk[^i-helped-6-522-people-get-off-blood-pressure-med-jjW5msWyFio].

# The 10-step protocol

1. **Confirm the diagnosis** with accurate home readings taken at rest, morning and evening for 7 days, averaged into a true baseline[^i-helped-6-522-people-get-off-blood-pressure-med-jjW5msWyFio].
2. **Target ultra-processed foods rather than sodium alone**, since he says these worsen insulin resistance and inflammation and displace potassium, magnesium, and fiber[^i-helped-6-522-people-get-off-blood-pressure-med-jjW5msWyFio].
3. **Add a daily potassium-rich food** (beans, lentils, leafy greens, potatoes, avocados, Greek yogurt) to relax vessel walls, with a caution for anyone with kidney disease to check with a doctor first[^i-helped-6-522-people-get-off-blood-pressure-med-jjW5msWyFio].
4. **Walk 10 minutes after every meal**, cited as reducing post-meal glucose spikes by up to 30% and improving vessel flexibility[^i-helped-6-522-people-get-off-blood-pressure-med-jjW5msWyFio].
5. **Add isometric training** (wall sits, planks, hand grips), which he cites a British Journal of Sports Medicine meta-analysis as finding more effective than cardio for lowering both blood pressure numbers[^i-helped-6-522-people-get-off-blood-pressure-med-jjW5msWyFio].
6. **Break up prolonged sitting** every 30-60 minutes to counter its effect of reducing blood flow and nitric oxide[^i-helped-6-522-people-get-off-blood-pressure-med-jjW5msWyFio].
7. **Prioritize sleep** with consistent sleep/wake times and screening for obstructive sleep apnea (snoring, breathing pauses, unrefreshing sleep, morning headaches, daytime sleepiness) as a hidden driver of resistant hypertension[^i-helped-6-522-people-get-off-blood-pressure-med-jjW5msWyFio].
8. **Avoid caffeine after noon**, since late-day caffeine is said to disrupt deep sleep and blunt the normal overnight blood pressure dip[^i-helped-6-522-people-get-off-blood-pressure-med-jjW5msWyFio].
9. **Treat alcohol "like a medication"** via a 2-4 week complete pause, since alcohol raises blood pressure directly and via stress hormones and sleep disruption[^i-helped-6-522-people-get-off-blood-pressure-med-jjW5msWyFio].
10. He suggests starting with just 3 items — correct measurement, post-meal walks, and isometric training — before layering in the rest[^i-helped-6-522-people-get-off-blood-pressure-med-jjW5msWyFio].

# Caveats

The "6,522 people" figure and most claims are the presenter's own clinical impressions rather than sourced data; the one specific citation (a British Journal of Sports Medicine meta-analysis on isometric training) is named without authors or sample size. The video promotes an Amazon affiliate link for a blood pressure cuff and a companion paid/second channel.
