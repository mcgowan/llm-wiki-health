# Overview

Dr. Mitch Rice's thesis is that visceral fat — the metabolically active fat wrapped around the abdominal organs — can be targeted independently of scale weight by repairing the gut microbiome with a specific prebiotic-plus-probiotic combination taken daily before lunch. The "800mg" in the title is the 0.8 g daily dose of the prebiotic xylooligosaccharide (XOS) used in the trial he builds the video around. He stresses that the probiotic (the "seed") and the prebiotic (the "fertilizer") must be taken together; most off-the-shelf gut products supply only one.[^800mg-before-lunch-is-the-easiest-way-to-kill-vi-lI6n3rz6RSM]

The evidence is a single randomized, placebo-controlled trial (which he attributes to the journal *Genes & Nutrition*) in overweight but otherwise healthy adults, measured by whole-body DEXA at baseline, 30, 60 and 90 days. Neither group lost weight, yet the supplement group showed a 37% reduction in visceral adipose tissue at day 60 and 35% at day 90, while placebo drifted slightly upward. Rice's clinical takeaway is that people who "fail" on the scale may still be winning where it matters metabolically, and that the effect plateaus around two months.[^800mg-before-lunch-is-the-easiest-way-to-kill-vi-lI6n3rz6RSM]

# The protocol

- **Prebiotic:** xylooligosaccharide, 0.8 g/day — chosen because it feeds beneficial bacteria efficiently at a low dose relative to other prebiotic fibers.[^800mg-before-lunch-is-the-easiest-way-to-kill-vi-lI6n3rz6RSM]
- **Probiotic:** *Bacillus subtilis* plus *Bacillus coagulans*, dosed in the billions of CFU/day. Both are spore-forming and therefore survive stomach acid far better than typical non-spore strains, so more live bacteria reach the intestine.[^800mg-before-lunch-is-the-easiest-way-to-kill-vi-lI6n3rz6RSM]
- **Timing and duration:** daily before lunch, for the full 60–90 days; the strongest measured effect was at day 60.[^800mg-before-lunch-is-the-easiest-way-to-kill-vi-lI6n3rz6RSM]
- **Sourcing:** because replicating the exact strain pairing with separate products is expensive, Rice recommends a single third-party-tested combination product containing XOS, *B. subtilis*, and additional *Bacillus*/*Lactobacillus* strains (affiliate-linked in the video description); he advises trusting only brands that list each ingredient's actual amount.[^800mg-before-lunch-is-the-easiest-way-to-kill-vi-lI6n3rz6RSM]

# Proposed mechanism

Rice explains the visceral-fat effect through short-chain fatty acids (SCFAs), chiefly butyrate and propionate, produced when the bacteria ferment the prebiotic fiber. He assigns them two jobs:[^800mg-before-lunch-is-the-easiest-way-to-kill-vi-lI6n3rz6RSM]

1. SCFAs prompt the gut to release GLP-1 — the same hormone that semaglutide drugs (Ozempic, Wegovy) mimic — producing a small natural release that curbs hunger and improves glucose handling.
2. SCFAs travel via the blood to visceral fat cells and dampen their inflammatory signalling. Because visceral fat is active tissue that continuously emits inflammatory signals, quieting it makes it easier for the body to break down and clear.

His framing: "it's not magic, it's bacteria making specific molecules that talk directly to your fat cells and your hormones."[^800mg-before-lunch-is-the-easiest-way-to-kill-vi-lI6n3rz6RSM]

# What undermines the protocol

- **Recent antibiotics** wipe out good bacteria along with bad; he recommends waiting 3–4 weeks after a course before starting the supplement.[^800mg-before-lunch-is-the-easiest-way-to-kill-vi-lI6n3rz6RSM]
- **Regular alcohol** reduces microbial diversity and damages the intestinal lining where the bacteria must colonize.[^800mg-before-lunch-is-the-easiest-way-to-kill-vi-lI6n3rz6RSM]
- **Artificial sweeteners** shift the microbiome in ways that specifically reduce the beneficial bacteria the plan depends on.[^800mg-before-lunch-is-the-easiest-way-to-kill-vi-lI6n3rz6RSM]

If 60 days pass with no drop in visceral fat or waistline, these three habits are the first things to audit.[^800mg-before-lunch-is-the-easiest-way-to-kill-vi-lI6n3rz6RSM]

# Caveats

The entire case rests on one trial described second-hand; the video gives no sample size, and the journal citation is by name only. The recommended product is affiliate-linked, and the video promotes Rice's paid 90-day visceral-fat community. The 35% figure is a relative change in DEXA-measured visceral adipose tissue, not weight loss. Treat this as a hypothesis worth checking against the primary paper rather than settled guidance.[^800mg-before-lunch-is-the-easiest-way-to-kill-vi-lI6n3rz6RSM]
