# Overview

Dr. Mitch Rice distinguishes visceral fat — fat that wraps around internal organs like the liver, pancreas, and intestines — from harmless subcutaneous fat, describing it as metabolically active tissue that continuously releases inflammatory chemicals and stress hormones, driving heart disease, type 2 diabetes, and early death, often in people who appear normal-weight[^fastest-protocol-to-erase-visceral-fat-doctor-ex-B6xtcGDoAqA]. His central claim is that because visceral fat is highly metabolically active, it responds unusually quickly to targeted lifestyle changes — often before the number on a scale moves — and he presents six specific, research-linked tactics intended to accelerate its loss[^fastest-protocol-to-erase-visceral-fat-doctor-ex-B6xtcGDoAqA].

# The six tactics

- **Eat protein before carbohydrates at each meal.** Citing a *Journal of Diabetes Care* study, Rice states this food-order change reduced post-meal blood sugar spikes by up to 37% and insulin spikes by up to 57%, by slowing gastric emptying and blunting how fast carbs are absorbed[^fastest-protocol-to-erase-visceral-fat-doctor-ex-B6xtcGDoAqA].
- **Isometric exercises** (wall sits, planks, hand-grip squeezes held 30-45 seconds, several rounds, 3x/week), which he says a *British Journal of Sports Medicine* study found reduced waist size more than standard cardio, by training blood vessels to relax under pressure and lowering stress hormones tied to visceral fat storage[^fastest-protocol-to-erase-visceral-fat-doctor-ex-B6xtcGDoAqA].
- **A consistent daily eating window of about 10 hours** (e.g., 8am-6pm), citing a *Journal of Cell Metabolism* study reporting significant visceral fat loss over 12 weeks from this schedule alone, attributed to insulin dropping to its lowest point during extended fasting stretches, prompting stored-fat burning[^fastest-protocol-to-erase-visceral-fat-doctor-ex-B6xtcGDoAqA].
- **Ending morning showers with 30-60 seconds of cold water**, 5-7 days a week, said to activate calorie-burning brown fat that draws fuel from visceral stores, citing a *Journal of Clinical Investigation* study on cold exposure and fat burning[^fastest-protocol-to-erase-visceral-fat-doctor-ex-B6xtcGDoAqA].
- **Getting outside within 30 minutes of waking**, for 5-10 minutes without sunglasses, to anchor cortisol's natural morning peak via the circadian rhythm; Rice cites *Journal of Biological Rhythms* research linking morning light exposure to lower baseline cortisol and reduced belly fat storage[^fastest-protocol-to-erase-visceral-fat-doctor-ex-B6xtcGDoAqA].
- **Nasal breathing during exercise**, which he says raises nitric oxide, shifting the body toward burning fat over sugar and keeping heart rate in the fat-burning "zone two" range; he cites a *Journal of Human Kinetics* study finding nasal breathing preserved fat burning better than mouth breathing at equal exercise intensity[^fastest-protocol-to-erase-visceral-fat-doctor-ex-B6xtcGDoAqA].

# Caveats

Each cited study (Journal of Diabetes Care, British Journal of Sports Medicine, Journal of Cell Metabolism, Journal of Clinical Investigation, Journal of Biological Rhythms, Journal of Human Kinetics) is named only briefly without sample sizes or full citations, so the strength and generalizability of these findings can't be verified from the video itself. The video includes an affiliate Amazon link for a specific berberine supplement and promotes a free "longevity blueprint" resource, both commercial elements layered onto the advice. Framing these six tactics as the "fastest" way to lose visceral fat is a marketing claim rather than a comparative clinical finding.
