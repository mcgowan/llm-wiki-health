# Overview

Dr. Mitch Rice argues that the composition of breakfast, not just its calorie content, shapes blood pressure for the rest of the day[^the-top-3-breakfast-foods-to-lower-blood-pressur-UztDaW6oaeQ]. His reasoning is that a breakfast causing a blood sugar spike-and-crash triggers stress hormones like adrenaline and cortisol, which tighten blood vessels and raise blood pressure, whereas a breakfast that keeps blood sugar steady keeps the nervous system calm and blood pressure lower[^the-top-3-breakfast-foods-to-lower-blood-pressur-UztDaW6oaeQ]. From this framework he recommends three specific breakfast foods — eggs, plain Greek yogurt, and berries — each said to lower blood pressure through a different mechanism[^the-top-3-breakfast-foods-to-lower-blood-pressur-UztDaW6oaeQ].

# The three foods

- **Eggs**: high in protein, which slows digestion, stabilizes blood sugar, and reduces stress-hormone release; he also states eggs contain choline, arginine, and other compounds that help relax blood vessels, and disputes the older view that dietary cholesterol from eggs raises blood pressure or heart risk[^the-top-3-breakfast-foods-to-lower-blood-pressur-UztDaW6oaeQ]. He suggests scrambled eggs with a salt substitute, olive oil, or avocado, avoiding pairing them with sugary sides like large amounts of toast, cereal, or sweet drinks[^the-top-3-breakfast-foods-to-lower-blood-pressur-UztDaW6oaeQ].
- **Plain Greek yogurt** (unsweetened, not flavored or fruit-on-bottom varieties): its protein and fat content blunt blood sugar spikes; its calcium and potassium support blood vessel contraction/relaxation and help the kidneys clear excess sodium; and as a fermented food, its probiotics are said to reduce inflammation and improve vessel flexibility via the gut-blood pressure connection[^the-top-3-breakfast-foods-to-lower-blood-pressur-UztDaW6oaeQ]. He states this effect is strongest in people with insulin resistance or prediabetes, and suggests adding berries, nuts, or cinnamon[^the-top-3-breakfast-foods-to-lower-blood-pressur-UztDaW6oaeQ].
- **Berries** (blueberries, raspberries): rich in flavonoids that he says raise nitric oxide production, relax blood vessels, reduce artery stiffness, and lower inflammation and oxidative stress; fresh or frozen both count, and he suggests pairing them with yogurt, a smoothie, or eggs[^the-top-3-breakfast-foods-to-lower-blood-pressur-UztDaW6oaeQ].

# Practical framework

Rice does not recommend eating all three foods every day, but suggests every breakfast include at least one of them, aiming for protein, fiber, and low sugar to keep blood sugar steady and the nervous system calm[^the-top-3-breakfast-foods-to-lower-blood-pressur-UztDaW6oaeQ]. He flags common breakfast foods he considers problematic for blood pressure, including sugary cereals, oatmeal, white toast with jam, and sweet fruit drinks, on the grounds that they cause blood sugar spikes and crashes that keep blood pressure elevated through the day[^the-top-3-breakfast-foods-to-lower-blood-pressur-UztDaW6oaeQ].
