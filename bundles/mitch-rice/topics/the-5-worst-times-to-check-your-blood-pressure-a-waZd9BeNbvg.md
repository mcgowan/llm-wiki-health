# Overview

Dr. Mitch Rice lists five common times when a home blood pressure reading is likely to be misleading, then gives a protocol for measuring blood pressure accurately.[^the-5-worst-times-to-check-your-blood-pressure-a-waZd9BeNbvg] His overarching claim is that blood pressure fluctuates constantly with physiological state, so the timing and technique of a reading — not just the monitor — determines whether the number reflects a person's true baseline.[^the-5-worst-times-to-check-your-blood-pressure-a-waZd9BeNbvg]

# Five times that distort readings

- Midday: cortisol and sympathetic nervous system activity peak during active waking hours, and ordinary movement adds further fluctuation; Rice cites a study he attributes to the Journal of Clinical Hypertension reporting that daytime readings run higher and more variable than readings taken under standardized resting conditions, with the highest rate of false hypertension classification.[^the-5-worst-times-to-check-your-blood-pressure-a-waZd9BeNbvg]
- Within 30-60 minutes of caffeine: caffeine stimulates the sympathetic nervous system and can raise systolic pressure by 5-15 points in sensitive individuals; he advises waiting 60-120 minutes after caffeine before checking.[^the-5-worst-times-to-check-your-blood-pressure-a-waZd9BeNbvg]
- With a full bladder: bladder-stretch receptors trigger sympathetic activation; a study Rice attributes to the Journal of Human Hypertension reports bladder fullness raising systolic pressure by 10-15 mmHg, enough to shift a reading into a higher category.[^the-5-worst-times-to-check-your-blood-pressure-a-waZd9BeNbvg]
- Within 60 minutes of hard exercise: blood pressure can remain elevated — or, in some people, be temporarily lower than baseline from post-exercise vasodilation — for up to an hour after intense effort, so neither extreme reflects a resting value.[^the-5-worst-times-to-check-your-blood-pressure-a-waZd9BeNbvg]
- Relying on a single reading: because blood pressure varies moment to moment, one reading can land well above or below a person's real average, causing unnecessary alarm or false reassurance.[^the-5-worst-times-to-check-your-blood-pressure-a-waZd9BeNbvg]

# The recommended protocol

Rice recommends sitting quietly for 5 minutes with feet flat, an empty bladder, and the arm at heart level, without talking; taking two readings one minute apart and recording the lower one; and repeating this daily for about 10 days to compute an average systolic and diastolic baseline. The two best windows he identifies are about 20 minutes after waking (before checking a phone or having coffee, and after using the bathroom) and in the evening before bed, once settled.[^the-5-worst-times-to-check-your-blood-pressure-a-waZd9BeNbvg]

# Caveats

The cited Journal of Clinical Hypertension and Journal of Human Hypertension findings are referenced without study names, authors, or sample sizes, so they cannot be verified as described. The video also promotes the creator's own free "blood pressure blueprint" resource.
