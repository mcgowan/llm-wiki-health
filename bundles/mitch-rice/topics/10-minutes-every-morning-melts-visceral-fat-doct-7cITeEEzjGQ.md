# Overview

Dr. Mitch Rice presents a 10-minute fasted-state morning routine he says targets visceral (organ-surrounding) fat specifically, without requiring intense exercise or calorie counting[^10-minutes-every-morning-melts-visceral-fat-doct-7cITeEEzjGQ]. The core premise is timing: performed within the first hour of waking, before eating, the body is already in a low-insulin state (favoring fat release) while cortisol is naturally rising (mobilizing energy), a combination he argues is primed for fat burning rather than storage — provided cortisol isn't further spiked by stress from immediately checking a phone or emails[^10-minutes-every-morning-melts-visceral-fat-doct-7cITeEEzjGQ].

# The protocol

- **Minute 1 — slow breathing**: sit quietly, eyes closed if comfortable, and take five slow breaths (4 seconds in through the nose, 6 seconds out through the mouth) before doing anything else. This is meant to activate the vagus nerve and prevent the natural morning cortisol rise from stacking into a stress response, which he says is linked to more visceral fat storage[^10-minutes-every-morning-melts-visceral-fat-doct-7cITeEEzjGQ].
- **Minutes 1-9 (about 8 minutes) — a brisk "zone two" walk**: an intensity at which conversation is possible but difficult and singing is not. He explains that at this moderate intensity the body relies predominantly on fat oxidation for fuel, whereas higher intensities shift the body toward burning glucose and glycogen instead[^10-minutes-every-morning-melts-visceral-fat-doct-7cITeEEzjGQ].
- **Final minute — cold water exposure**: a cold shower or cold-water submersion, said to activate brown adipose tissue, a fat type that burns calories to generate heat, raising metabolic rate for a period afterward. He cites a Journal of Clinical Investigation study finding this effect was stronger when cold exposure was paired with fasted exercise than with either alone[^10-minutes-every-morning-melts-visceral-fat-doct-7cITeEEzjGQ].
- **Optional add-on — matcha green tea** before the walk: its EGCG catechin is said, per a 12-week clinical trial using CT scans, to reduce visceral fat, and separately to modestly boost fat oxidation during exercise in a fasted state[^10-minutes-every-morning-melts-visceral-fat-doct-7cITeEEzjGQ].

He claims consistent use of the full routine should produce "substantial" visceral fat loss within 30 days[^10-minutes-every-morning-melts-visceral-fat-doct-7cITeEEzjGQ].

# Caveats

The video cites three separate studies (on slow breathing and cortisol, on EGCG/matcha and visceral fat, and on cold exposure and brown fat) but names no authors, journals for two of them, sample sizes, or effect sizes beyond vague descriptions, so none can be verified from the video alone. The 30-day "substantial" fat-loss claim is presented as a personal guarantee rather than a cited result. The video includes an Amazon affiliate link for a specific matcha brand and promotes the creator's own free "Metabolic Blueprint" resource.
