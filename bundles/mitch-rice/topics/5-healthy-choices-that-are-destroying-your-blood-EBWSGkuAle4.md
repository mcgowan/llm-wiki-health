# Overview

Dr. Mitch Rice argues that some patients follow standard blood-pressure advice yet see no improvement because of a subtler flaw in how they execute it, and describes five common "healthy" habits that he says quietly keep the sympathetic nervous system activated and blood pressure elevated.[^5-healthy-choices-that-are-destroying-your-blood-EBWSGkuAle4] The unifying theme he offers is that each habit, done in its typical form, triggers a stress response (via blood sugar spikes, unbalanced electrolytes, cortisol amplification, reduced nitric oxide, or anticipatory anxiety) that keeps arteries from relaxing.[^5-healthy-choices-that-are-destroying-your-blood-EBWSGkuAle4]

# The five habits and fixes

- **Morning smoothies**: fruit- and juice-heavy smoothies deliver a fast, fiber-poor sugar load that spikes insulin, causing the kidneys to retain sodium and arteries to constrict. Fix: add protein (Greek yogurt, nut butter, protein powder), favor greens/berries over tropical fruit, and use water or unsweetened milk instead of juice as the base.[^5-healthy-choices-that-are-destroying-your-blood-EBWSGkuAle4]
- **Cutting salt without adding potassium**: blood pressure is framed as a sodium-to-potassium balance issue — potassium helps kidneys excrete excess sodium, so cutting sodium alone without enough potassium leaves little effect. Fix: include a potassium-rich food (beans/lentils, avocado, sweet potato, salmon, Greek yogurt, leafy greens) at every meal, and also raise magnesium intake, since both minerals counteract sodium's effects.[^5-healthy-choices-that-are-destroying-your-blood-EBWSGkuAle4]
- **Fasted morning coffee**: caffeine taken on an empty stomach during the natural post-waking cortisol peak (the "cortisol awakening response") amplifies cortisol further, prolonging sympathetic activation and arterial constriction. Fix: wait about 90 minutes after waking before the first coffee, and eat some protein (e.g., nuts or a hard-boiled egg) beforehand.[^5-healthy-choices-that-are-destroying-your-blood-EBWSGkuAle4]
- **Mouth breathing (exercise and sleep)**: nasal breathing generates nitric oxide that helps relax blood vessels, while mouth breathing does not; chronic nighttime mouth breathing is linked to reduced overnight blood pressure "dipping," a marker of cardiovascular risk. Fix: practice nasal-only breathing during exercise, and consider nasal strips or mouth tape at night (with a sleep apnea evaluation if snoring or unrefreshing sleep is present).[^5-healthy-choices-that-are-destroying-your-blood-EBWSGkuAle4]
- **Checking blood pressure too often**: frequent self-checks create anticipatory anxiety that itself raises readings, producing a self-reinforcing "checking spiral"; a single reading reflects momentary factors more than true baseline.[^5-healthy-choices-that-are-destroying-your-blood-EBWSGkuAle4]
