# Overview

Dr. Mitch Rice argues that commonly recommended "heart-healthy" foods are often incomplete or counterproductive, and instead names five foods he says have evidence for protecting against, or even partially reversing, arterial plaque buildup, plus foods that undermine artery health despite a healthy reputation.[^the-1-best-meal-to-unclog-your-arteries-protect-6mlZjQDz2iQ] His underlying model is that damaged, rough artery lining traps circulating LDL particles, which then oxidize and trigger an inflammatory cascade that grows plaque; each recommended food is said to interrupt a different step of that process.[^the-1-best-meal-to-unclog-your-arteries-protect-6mlZjQDz2iQ]

# Five foods for artery health

- **Cooked tomatoes.** Rich in the antioxidant lycopene, which he says neutralizes oxidative stress before it triggers plaque-building inflammation; cooked tomatoes are said to provide more lycopene than raw. He notes tomatoes are a nightshade and may not suit people with certain autoimmune sensitivities.[^the-1-best-meal-to-unclog-your-arteries-protect-6mlZjQDz2iQ]
- **Nuts, seeds, and legumes.** Contain phytosterols (plant compounds resembling cholesterol) that compete with dietary cholesterol for gut absorption, prompting the liver to pull more LDL from the bloodstream to compensate. He cites meta-analyses showing phytosterols can lower LDL by around 14 mg/dL.[^the-1-best-meal-to-unclog-your-arteries-protect-6mlZjQDz2iQ]
- **Eggs.** He argues the older concern about dietary cholesterol in eggs raising blood cholesterol has been largely overstated, and that egg yolks' choline, lutein, and zeaxanthin instead help reduce LDL oxidation, the step that actually drives plaque growth. He recommends poaching or boiling over frying in butter or processed oils.[^the-1-best-meal-to-unclog-your-arteries-protect-6mlZjQDz2iQ]
- **Flavonoid-rich foods (including dark chocolate, berries, onions, apples, green tea).** Flavonoids are said to make the artery lining "less sticky" so fewer inflammatory cells attach. He cites a meta-analysis of nine studies linking three or more cups of green tea daily to a 21% lower stroke risk.[^the-1-best-meal-to-unclog-your-arteries-protect-6mlZjQDz2iQ]
- **Fatty fish (omega-3s).** EPA and DHA are said to calm the immune response and boost nitric oxide production, making existing plaques more stable and less likely to rupture — the event that actually triggers most heart attacks and strokes. He cites the MESA trial linking higher DHA levels to a 12% lower rate of carotid plaque progression, recommending fatty fish 1-3 times weekly or a fish oil supplement.[^the-1-best-meal-to-unclog-your-arteries-protect-6mlZjQDz2iQ]

# Foods to limit, and a bonus supplement

He warns that processed carbohydrates and added sugar — including flavored yogurts, granola/breakfast bars, and many breakfast cereals — repeatedly spike blood sugar in a way that damages artery lining despite a "healthy" label, and recommends checking labels for added sugar.[^the-1-best-meal-to-unclog-your-arteries-protect-6mlZjQDz2iQ] As a bonus, he recommends aged garlic extract (distinct from raw garlic), citing two randomized controlled trials showing it can stabilize vulnerable plaques and slow coronary artery calcification progression over one year, alongside blood-pressure benefits.[^the-1-best-meal-to-unclog-your-arteries-protect-6mlZjQDz2iQ]

# Caveats

Several claims cite named studies or meta-analyses (MESA trial, a nine-study meta-analysis on green tea, "two randomized controlled trials" on aged garlic) but omit authors, years, or full details needed to verify them independently. The video includes affiliate Amazon links for matcha, fish oil, and aged garlic extract supplements.
