# Overview

Dr. Mitch Rice presents a 10-minute daily "blood pressure reset" routine intended to lower blood pressure in adults over 60 by roughly 8-15 points, without exercise beyond the routine itself, dieting, or purchases.[^the-10-min-daily-habit-to-lower-blood-pressure-i-mOmf3nzt3y4] The routine is built from three timed blocks that each target a different physiological driver of high blood pressure: arterial stiffness, an overactive sympathetic nervous system, and mild dehydration.[^the-10-min-daily-habit-to-lower-blood-pressure-i-mOmf3nzt3y4]

# The three-part routine

- Minutes 0-6: isometric exercise (wall sits and planks, or hand-grip/squat holds as substitutes), done in roughly 2-minute blocks. Rice describes aging arteries as stiffer "garden hoses" that force the heart to pump harder, and says sustained muscular tension during a hold retrains blood vessels to dilate more once released, citing "clinical studies and meta-analyses" reporting an average ~10-point drop in systolic pressure — said to be comparable to a prescription medication.[^the-10-min-daily-habit-to-lower-blood-pressure-i-mOmf3nzt3y4]
- Minutes 6-8: a "vagus nerve reset" via resonance breathing — inhaling through the nose for 5 seconds and exhaling through the mouth for 5 seconds, six breaths per minute, for about 2 minutes. This is said to raise heart-rate variability and baroreceptor sensitivity and counteract age-related sympathetic dominance.[^the-10-min-daily-habit-to-lower-blood-pressure-i-mOmf3nzt3y4]
- Minutes 8-10: hydration — slowly sipping 12-16 ounces of water over about 2 minutes, on the reasoning that mild dehydration lowers blood volume and triggers compensatory vessel constriction that raises blood pressure.[^the-10-min-daily-habit-to-lower-blood-pressure-i-mOmf3nzt3y4]

# Habit-formation tips

To help the routine stick, Rice recommends anchoring it to an existing daily habit (for example, right after coffee, tooth-brushing, or morning medication), tracking it on a calendar, starting with 5 minutes if 10 feels like too much, and doing it with a partner for accountability.[^the-10-min-daily-habit-to-lower-blood-pressure-i-mOmf3nzt3y4]

# Caveats

The claimed point-drops (8-15 overall; roughly 10 from isometrics alone) are attributed to unnamed "clinical studies and meta-analyses" without citations or sample sizes, so they cannot be independently verified. The video also promotes the creator's own paid "blood pressure reset guide" product.
