# Overview

Dr. Mitch Rice argues that urine color is an underused diagnostic signal and walks through five colors that can flag underlying medical problems, ranging from harmless dietary effects to genuine emergencies[^5-urine-warning-signs-87-of-patients-miss-IiVFicgp4ig]. His central message is that any red or pink discoloration should prompt a same-day doctor visit, since blood is not supposed to leave the vasculature, while other color changes fall on a spectrum from benign to urgent depending on context and accompanying symptoms[^5-urine-warning-signs-87-of-patients-miss-IiVFicgp4ig].

# The five warning signs

- Red or pink urine (blood) warrants urgent same-day evaluation; it is most often caused by infection, kidney stones, or vigorous exercise, but he shares a case of a middle-aged patient whose painless pink urine turned out to be an early, successfully treated bladder tumor[^5-urine-warning-signs-87-of-patients-miss-IiVFicgp4ig]. Two common false alarms are beeturia (a harmless genetic inability to fully break down betanin from beets, said to affect roughly 1 in 10 people) and the UTI drug phenazopyridine (Azo), both of which discolor urine without any actual bleeding[^5-urine-warning-signs-87-of-patients-miss-IiVFicgp4ig].
- Dark, cola-colored urine after intense or unaccustomed exercise can simply reflect dehydration, but persistent dark urine accompanied by weakness, soreness, or nausea can indicate rhabdomyolysis, a breakdown of muscle tissue that releases myoglobin into the urine, and should prompt emergency care[^5-urine-warning-signs-87-of-patients-miss-IiVFicgp4ig].
- Cloudy urine alone is usually benign dehydration or sediment, but cloudiness combined with burning, urgency, or lower abdominal pain suggests a urinary tract infection, which he flags as especially notable in men because UTIs are comparatively rare in that group[^5-urine-warning-signs-87-of-patients-miss-IiVFicgp4ig].
- Persistent (not one-off) foamy urine can signal protein leaking from the kidneys, an early marker of kidney strain or chronic kidney disease, best checked with a first-morning urine sample[^5-urine-warning-signs-87-of-patients-miss-IiVFicgp4ig].
- Purple urine, the rarest sign discussed, results from "purple urine bag syndrome," seen almost exclusively in long-term catheter users, when bacterial breakdown of tryptophan produces indigo and indirubin pigments; it signals a catheter-associated bacterial infection rather than a separate danger[^5-urine-warning-signs-87-of-patients-miss-IiVFicgp4ig].

# Caveats

This summarizes a single YouTube video's claims from one physician; the specific figures cited (such as "87%" in the title and "1 in 10" for beeturia) are not attributed to any named study, and the bladder-tumor case is a single anecdotal patient example rather than systematic evidence[^5-urine-warning-signs-87-of-patients-miss-IiVFicgp4ig].
