# Overview

Dr. Mitch Rice argues that pancreatic cancer's low survival rate is largely a timing problem — most cases are diagnosed after the disease has already spread — and presents seven subtle bodily signs he says can appear months before diagnosis but are commonly dismissed as aging, stress, or a bad week.[^your-body-warns-you-months-before-pancreatic-can-YXTijNcGQgc] His central message is that no single sign is diagnostic on its own, but two or more of these signs persisting for weeks to months together warrant medical evaluation.[^your-body-warns-you-months-before-pancreatic-can-YXTijNcGQgc]

# The seven signs

The signs, with the mechanism given for each: (1) sudden new-onset type 2 diabetes after age 50, proposed to result from a tumor disrupting insulin-producing pancreatic beta cells rather than the typical slow progression from prediabetes; (2) unexplained weight loss of roughly 5% of body weight over 6–12 months, attributed to impaired digestive enzyme production and a higher metabolic rate; (3) jaundice (yellowing skin/eyes), from a tumor in the head of the pancreas compressing the adjacent bile duct and causing bilirubin to build up in the blood; (4) pale, greasy, foul-smelling, floating stool (steatorrhea), from blocked digestive enzymes leaving dietary fat undigested; (5) persistent upper-abdominal pain radiating to the back that worsens after eating or lying flat and eases with leaning forward; (6) new-onset depression or sudden mood change, proposed to stem from inflammatory and hormonal signaling from the tumor itself rather than an emotional reaction to feeling unwell; and (7) an unexplained blood clot (DVT or pulmonary embolism), linked to tumors increasing the blood's clotting tendency, a pattern referred to in the medical literature as Trousseau syndrome.[^your-body-warns-you-months-before-pancreatic-can-YXTijNcGQgc]

# What to watch for

He stresses that each sign individually has many more common, benign explanations, and that intentional weight loss, situational abdominal pain, or a one-off greasy stool are not cause for alarm — but a cluster of two or more of these signs persisting for weeks to months, especially new-onset diabetes or unexplained weight loss, should prompt a medical workup.[^your-body-warns-you-months-before-pancreatic-can-YXTijNcGQgc]

# Caveats

The claim that "multiple studies" link new-onset depression to pancreatic cancer diagnosis is not accompanied by any citation, author, or sample size. The video closes with a pitch for a paid one-on-one "personal doctor" waitlist with the presenter.
